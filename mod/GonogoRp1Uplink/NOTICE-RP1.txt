GonogoRp1Uplink integrates with RP-1 / Realistic Progression One (the RP-0
plugin, https://github.com/KSP-RO/RP-0) entirely at ARM'S LENGTH: it reads
RP-1's space-centre runtime state (SpaceCenterManagement and the launch
complex, build queue, launch pad, rollout, research and Confidence objects it
holds) through reflection ONLY. It does NOT link the RP-0 assembly at build
time, does NOT derive from RP-1 types, and bundles NO RP-1 source or assets.
