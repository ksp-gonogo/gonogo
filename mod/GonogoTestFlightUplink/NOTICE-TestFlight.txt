GonogoTestFlightUplink reads TestFlight state ENTIRELY by runtime reflection
(see TestFlightReflection.cs). This assembly does not link, bundle, or derive
from TestFlight (TestFlightCore.dll / TestFlightAPI.dll) - it holds no
compile-time reference to them and copies none of their code. GonogoTestFlightUplink
itself is MIT-licensed (see LICENSING.md).

TestFlight is an independent Kerbal Space Program mod by its own authors,
distributed under its own licence. This notice retains its attribution; no part
of TestFlight is redistributed here. Install TestFlight separately (via CKAN or
its own distribution) for this uplink to have anything to reflect against.
