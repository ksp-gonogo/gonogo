GonogoFerramAerospaceResearchUplink integrates with Ferram Aerospace Research
(FAR, https://github.com/dkavolis/Ferram-Aerospace-Research) entirely at ARM'S
LENGTH: it reads FAR's per-vessel flight information (the FlightGUI vessel
module's VesselFlightInfo, the airspeed helper beside it, and the voxelisation
state on FARVesselAero) through reflection ONLY. It does NOT link
FerramAerospaceResearch.dll at build time, does NOT derive from FAR types, and
bundles NO FAR source or assets.

FAR ships a LICENSE file at its repository root that automated licence
detection cannot classify, and the mod is widely understood to be
copyleft-family. This uplink therefore treats it as copyleft-or-unknown and
does not link it. Because none of FAR's code is incorporated (arm's-length
reflection over the running mod's members), no ShareAlike obligation is
triggered, and GonogoFerramAerospaceResearchUplink is licensed MIT, in line
with the rest of gonogo.

This notice is retained to attribute FAR and its authors for the aerodynamic
model this uplink reads: the angle-of-attack and sideslip geometry, the
wing-area-weighted stall fraction, the whole-vessel force breakdown and its
coefficients, the indicated and equivalent airspeed derivations, and the
ballistic-coefficient and terminal-velocity estimates.

FAR contributors: https://github.com/dkavolis/Ferram-Aerospace-Research/graphs/contributors
