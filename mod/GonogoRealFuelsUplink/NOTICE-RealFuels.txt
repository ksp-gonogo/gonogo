GonogoRealFuelsUplink integrates with RealFuels
(https://github.com/KSP-RO/RealFuels) entirely at ARM'S LENGTH: it reads
RealFuels' runtime state (ModuleEnginesRF ignition budget, UllageSet propellant
settling, ModuleFuelTanks boiloff) through reflection ONLY. It does NOT link
the RealFuels assembly at build time, does NOT derive from RealFuels types, and
bundles NO RealFuels source or assets.

Its ullage simulation is based on HoneyFox's EngineIgnitor, reused by RealFuels
under the MIT licence.

Because this uplink incorporates none of RealFuels' code (arm's-length
reflection over the running plugin's members), the ShareAlike obligation is not
triggered, and GonogoRealFuelsUplink is licensed MIT, in line with the rest of
gonogo. This notice is retained to attribute RealFuels and its authors for the
engine-realism model this uplink reads.
