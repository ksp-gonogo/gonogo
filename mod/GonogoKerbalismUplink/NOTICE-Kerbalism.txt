GonogoKerbalismUplink reaches Kerbalism entirely by runtime reflection
(KerbalismReflection.cs). It does NOT link Kerbalism's assembly and does NOT
derive from its types — the same arm's-length pattern GonogoRealAntennasUplink
uses for RealAntennas.

Kerbalism is authored by Sir Mortimer and the Kerbalism contributors and
distributed under the Unlicense (public domain dedication). Kerbalism and
Community Resource Pack (CRP) are the property of their respective authors; this
uplink neither bundles nor modifies them. It only observes their public runtime
surface (KERBALISM.API / KERBALISM.Features / KERBALISM.DB / ProcessController
PartModules) when they happen to be installed in the player's KSP.

Kerbalism: https://github.com/Kerbalism/Kerbalism
