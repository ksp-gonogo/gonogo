GonogoAvionicsUplink integrates with RP-1 / Realistic Progression One (the
RP-0 plugin, https://github.com/KSP-RO/RP-0) entirely at ARM'S LENGTH: it reads
RP-1's avionics runtime state (ModuleAvionics / ModuleProceduralAvionics
CurrentMassLimit) through reflection ONLY. It does NOT link the RP-0 assembly
at build time, does NOT derive from RP-1 types, and bundles NO RP-1 source or
assets.
